# ARCLINE STUDIO — Creative Direction

## Big Creative Concept
THE SPACE BETWEEN

The experience explores relationships between:
- line and volume
- light and shadow
- solid and empty
- structure and softness
- architecture and living

## Creative Principle
The website itself should feel like a designed space.

## Visual Territory
Architectural editorial + contemporary warmth.

## Primary Keywords
Architectural, tactile, restrained, editorial, contemporary.

## Secondary Keywords
Warm, quiet, precise, atmospheric, human.

## Never
Flashy, ornate, generic luxury, corporate, techy, over-designed.

## Experience Formula
Arrival → Orientation → Exploration → Material → Method → Trust → Enquiry.

## Visual Formula
Architectural grid + editorial typography + tactile photography + material palette + negative space + restrained motion.

## Photography Direction
Editorial architectural photography emphasizing geometry, light, negative space, material, scale, perspective and atmosphere. Avoid stock posing, excessive filters and catalogue-like styling.

## Graphic Language
Thin rules, project numbering, technical labels, coordinates, strong 12-column grid, generous margins and asymmetry.

## UI Direction
Minimal, flat and precise. Avoid SaaS-style pills and excessive rounded cards.

## Motion Direction
Physical spatial movement: image reveals, text entrances, project transitions, subtle hover states and line animations. Photography + typography + motion should lead; 3D is optional and restrained.
