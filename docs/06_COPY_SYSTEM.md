## Philosophy
### Heading
Good interiors begin with understanding space.

### Body
Before colour, furniture or finishes, there is space. We study how it moves, how light enters it and how people live within it—then build the design from there.

## Projects
### Heading
A study of space, material and light.

### Supporting

## Materiality
### Heading
Material is not decoration. It defines atmosphere.

### Body
Stone, timber, fabric, metal and light become part of the spatial language—chosen not individually, but for how they work together.

## Design Principles
### Heading
Four things shape every space.

FORM — Proportion creates the foundation of a room.
MATERIAL — Texture gives architecture its physical character.
LIGHT — Natural and considered light shape how a space changes through the day.
LIFE — A well-designed space responds to the people who inhabit it.

## Services
### Heading
From spatial idea to considered interior.

Residential Interiors — Complete interior direction for apartments, houses and contemporary residences.
Spatial Planning — Layouts developed around movement, function, proportion and everyday use.
Material Direction — A coherent language of surfaces, textures, fixtures and finishes.
Design Detailing — The smaller decisions that bring the larger design idea into focus.

## Approach
### Heading
A considered process.

Understand — We begin with the space, the brief and the way it needs to be lived in.
Define — We establish the spatial direction, material language and design intent.
Develop — Plans, materials, lighting and details come together into a coherent system.
Resolve — We refine the decisions that make the final space feel complete.

## Studio
### Heading

### Body
