Each project gets unique title and description while remaining clearly conceptual.

Example:

Description:
A conceptual New Delhi apartment exploring proportion, warm oak, limestone and restrained contemporary interiors.

## Technical SEO

- One clear H1 per page
- Logical H2/H3 hierarchy
- Page titles
- Meta descriptions
- Canonicals
- Open Graph metadata
- Sitemap
- Robots.txt
- Appropriate structured data
- Internal linking
- 404 page
- Image alt text
- Responsive images
- Performance testing

## Images
Use descriptive filenames, optimized WebP/AVIF where appropriate, responsive sizing and lazy loading below the fold. Hero imagery should be prioritized.

## Local SEO
Use Delhi, Gurugram/Gurgaon, Noida and Delhi NCR context naturally. Never invent a physical address or create a fake Google Business Profile.

## Structured Data
Potentially use Organization, WebSite, BreadcrumbList and appropriate creative-work/project representations. Never fabricate ratings, reviews, awards or other unsupported facts.

## Social
Use Open Graph and controlled social preview images, ideally around 1200×630.

## Content Expansion
Future design-led educational content may target topics such as apartment planning, material selection, natural light and contemporary interiors. Blog expansion is not part of MVP.

## Concept Disclosure
Keep ARCLINE clearly identified as a fictional concept project.
